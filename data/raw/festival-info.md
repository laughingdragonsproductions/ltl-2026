## Festival FAQs

 What's the best way to get to the festival? 

Louder Than Life takes place at the Kentucky Expo Center in Louisville, KY. The Kentucky Expo Center is conveniently located at the junction of I-65 and I-264 and has multiple entrances to paved parking lots. The grounds are located adjacent to the 1-65 and the Louisville International Airport. Please visit our [Parking Info](https://louderthanlifefestival.com/parking-info/) to learn all the ways to get to Louder Than Life. 

 What about the weather? 

Louder Than Life is a r ain-or-shine event, however, there are several instances in which the festival may be paused, evacuated, or canceled due to inclement weather, including(but not limited to): severe thunderstorms, lightning in the festival radius, excessive wind, hail, flooding, or as directed by local emergency personnel. We recommend fans review our [Health & Safety](https://louderthanlifefestival.com/health-safety/) page, so you can be prepared to get to safety in the event of inclement weather.

 What ages are allowed? 

This is an all ages event. All attendees, regardless of age, are required to purchase a full price festival pass. We strongly encourage parents to bring hearing and sun protection for their children. **Please note, however, that the festival features an adult environment, which may include strong language, mature themes, and behavior not suited for younger attendees. Parental discretion is advised.**

 Will re-entry be allowed at the festival? 

Re-entry into the festival is not permitted unless you are camping on site and have a camping wristband or purchased a Hotel + Admission P ackage through JamPack. Once you have entered the venue each day, you will not be allowed to leave and come back in. Please plan accordingly. If there is an emergency and you must leave, please speak to a supervisor at the gate.

 My friend is terminally ill and wants to attend the show. Can you do something special for them? 

We work with Living The Dream Foundation, a great company that will do their best to accommodate these special fans when possible. They will screen all requests and reach out if there is an opportunity! Find out more and to contact LTD Foundation[HERE](https://ltdfoundation.org/).

 Where can I go to pick up lost items during & after the event is over? 

Lost & Found is located at the Lost & Found Booths near the festival entrances. In addition to visiting the Info Booths, there will be a link available on our website and festival mobile app during show days to search our found items database to see if your lost item has been turned in or submit a claim for your lost item. Post-show Lost & Found location will be shared on our social media during the show weekend.

 Which stage and what time will bands be playing? 

A full schedule with set times will be posted about two weeks before the show via our socials and mobile app.

 What time do gates open daily? 

Gates are scheduled to open at 10:45am daily, however, this is subject to change.

 Will alcohol be sold? 

Yes. Beer, wine,and mixed drinks will be sold to those 21 or older with a valid government issued photo ID.

 Will there be a sober living program? 

Yes! Sober Life is a supportive and inclusive initiative designed for attendees who choose to enjoy the festival without the use of alcohol or drugs. The program offers a safe and welcoming environment with various activities, resources, and support to ensure everyone can have a fun and memorable festival experience. This program will also host(3) recovery-style meetings each day.

 Will there be lockers? 

Lockers will be available in the General Admission (both Highland and Cardinal entrances), VIP, and Top Shelf VIP areas. You can reserve a locker in advance [**HERE**](https://secure.mobilecharginglockers.com/affiliate/website/event/louder-than-life-locker-rental-2026) or on-site at the festival, subject to availability. Each locker measures 8”H x 12”W x 18”D and comes with a universal phone charger. Lockers can be reserved for a single day or all 4 days, and if you book for all 4 days, you can leave your items securely stored overnight!

 How can I submit my band? 

We are not currently accepting fan or band submissions for signed artists. If you have an unsigned band, we have an amazing way to get on our radar via the DWPresents channel on Twitch. That Space Zebra show runs contests year round to help us find the best unsigned bands for a chance to battle for the big stage a DWP Festival. Submit an application today [HERE](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScES7yQqxy1VlK4KnpQwuZNaHEuL-kftyv9q5K472qfKYJIvQ/viewform).

 Do you offer any special hotel rates for fans? 

Yes! W e’ve partnered with our friends at Jampack, to offer packages that include 4-Day Festival Passes and discounted hotel reservation. Hotel packages will be available to purchase during the 2026 E arly B ird Pres ale.

 How can I apply to be a vendor? 

If you are interested in becoming a food vendor at Louder Than Life 2026, please apply [HERE](https://soho_concessions_application.formstack.com/forms/louder_than_life_2026_vendor_application). Please note the application is available from a desktop computer only and not phone. We recommend filling out using the Chrome internet browser. We are not currently accepting marketplace vendor applications at this time.

 What are my options for breastfeeding or pumping? 

 Anyone needing a space to breastfeed or pump can visit the Info Booth/Access Center near the main entrance of the festival. Please note that this area is not private, so consider bringing any coverings or items you need for comfort. You are welcome to bring necessary supplies, such as a pump, small soft-sided cooler, ice packs, and a cover, into the venue. However, please be aware that we are not responsible for any lost or damaged items. If you have any questions feel free to reach out to [access@louderthanlifefestival.com](mailto:access@louderthanlifefestival.com "mailto:access@louderthanlifefestival.com"). 

 Are there any opportunities to volunteer? 

**Clean Vibes:**We are excited to have[Clean Vibes](https://cleanvibes.com/) return to Louder Than Life to keep Louisville clean. Clean Vibes will be accepting volunteers to help with the cleanup and recycling effort in exchange for admission to the festival. Volunteer applications will be available closer to the event. Submit your volunteer application today, [HERE](https://cleanvibes.volunteerlocal.com/volunteer/).**Fan-To-Fan Services:**We’re looking for friendly, enthusiastic individuals who are excited to be part of something bigger. Our Guest Services Booths and Access Centers play a key role in creating a welcoming, inclusive, and unforgettable experience for every fan who walks through our gates. If you’re passionate about helping others and want to make a real impact at the festival, we’d love to have you on the team. The Fan-To-Fan Services volunteer sign up has closed for 2026.

1.   Prescription medication must be in the pharmacy bottle with a valid & current prescription stating: your name, medication name and dosage. Our medical staff will verify this if necessary.
2.   You must have a valid ID ready to present if requested.
3.   The name on your valid ID and the name on the valid & current prescription on the bottle must match.
4.   You are entering the event with sufficient medication for one day.
5.   Please Note: Drugs of any kind (incl. cannabis, cannabis products) are prohibited. Staff at the entry gates will verify the contents of any medically necessary materials. Out of concern for the festival staff who handle the trash, please dispose of your medical waste and syringes in the proper disposal, which is located at the medical tent. Please do not dispose of these items in other trash bins at the festival.

Please direct any further questions to access@louderthanlifefestival.com.

## Cashless FAQs

 What payment types are included under 'cashless'? 

Credit/Debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Android Pay, Tap to Pay, and registered RFID wristband are all forms of accepted‘Cashless’ payment types. 

 Can I still pay with a Credit/Debit card? 

Yes, you can still use your credit or debit card at any point of sale terminal.

 What forms of payment will be accepted? 

Credit and debit Card payments will be accepted in addition to RFID wristband, Tap to Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

 Will all vendors accept RFID bands? 

Both card payments and RFID wristbands will be accepted by all food and beverage vendors, and merch andise tents. 

 Will there be reverse ATMs? 

Yes! We will have reverse ATMs on site, for you to exchange your cash into a prepaid Master card. There is a $5 minimum and $5 00 maximum transaction, with a $4.95 fee per card.

 Will cash still be accepted in parking? 

While cash is not accepted inside the venue, cash will still be accepted in day of show parking lots.

 Will cash still be accepted in camping (general store, RV pump out, box office)? 

Our General Stores in camping and the Box Office will be cashless. We will have reverse ATMs nearby for your use! If you are needing your RV pumped out, they will only accept cash.

 What happens if I don’t use all of my money loaded onto my Pre paid card, issued by reverse ATM? 

 When using a reverse ATM, you will be issued a card which works as a Mastercard Gift Card, which can be used anywhere Mastercard is accepted.
