Where can I buy passes in advance?

You can purchase passes online via [Front Gate Tickets](https://louderthanlife.frontgatetickets.com/), [StubHub](https://www.stubhub.com/performer/1495124/?PCID=O?PCID=ODRkj43ntbm&t=t1.odr.ODRkj43ntbm) or our friends at[Jampack](https://louderthanlifefestival.com/passes/#hotel-packages) where you can purchase Hotel + Admission bundles. My friend and I purchased our passes the same day. They received their order, but I have not received mine yet. Should I be worried?

Not to worry! Passes are fulfilled and shipped in batches over several weeks. If your order is being shipped within the U.S., you will receive a tracking number once your pass has been mailed. Passes ship approximately 5-6 weeks before the festival.

When will I receive my passes?

All wristbands are shipped 4-5 weeks prior to the festival.Your passes will be mailed out to you. As each order is fulfilled, an email will be sent with a tracking number. Emails will come from noreply@frontgatetickets.com and sometimes end up in spam folders; we advise checking there as well as adding this email to your safe-sender list. For those who purchased passes after the deadline, please bring your order confirmation and government-issued photo ID to the Box Office to receive your wristband. If you have questions about receiving your order, shipping address, tracking info, etc. please email us at[info@louderthanlifefestival.com](mailto:info@louderthanlifefestival.com).

What is the Exacta pass?

Wanna go the extra mile and pack your September with all your favorite music? With the Exacta Pass, you get to double down on back-to-back weekends for the Biggest 8 Days of Music. The Exacta pass is a 4-Day General Admission Pass to both Bourbon & Beyond and Louder Than Life for one low price.

When will I receive my Exacta pass?

Passes will be fulfilled as separate event wristbands, for each festival, within 4-5 weeks prior to each festival.

How can I update my shipping address or billing info?

To update your shipping or payment information, please log into your Front Gate account [HERE](https://louderthanlife.frontgatetickets.com/) by hitting “sign in” at the top of the page! From there, you’ll be able to view and manage your order.If you need any assistance, please email us at info@louderthanlifefestival.com.

Can someone else pick up my passes at will call?

Yes, someone other than the original purchaser can pick up the wristbands. However, they will need to bring the original order confirmation email, a copy of the original purchaser’s government-issued photo ID, and written consent from the original purchaser stating they can pick up the wristbands.

What type of ID is accepted at the box office?

The B ox O ffice will accept a State issued Driver’s license, a government-issued Non-Driver ID Card, Military ID, or Passport. Please make sure to have one of these forms of ID with you!

Can I get a refund on my passes?

No. We do not offer refunds or exchanges on pass sales. All artists and scheduled performance times are subject to change. The Festival is outdoors and is held rain or shine.

What happens if I default on my layaway?

If you miss your payment, you will have up to a 30-day grace period to pay your next installment before your order is subject to cancellation and a non-refundable $50 fee + $15 termination fee.

Can I upgrade my single day to 4-Day or GA to VIP?

Can I split my weekend pass up to different people every day?

No, your pass is an RFID wristband and a 4-Day P ass is not transferable across multiple attendees.

What is a waitlist?

When a show is not currently on sale(ex: presale or a sold out pass type), items may be listed on a waitlist to offer festival or camping passes that aren’t available to the public. Due to high demand and limited availability, pricing will be dynamic and is subject to change.

Do all passes have a waitlist?

No. D ue to availability, not every pass will offer a waitlist.

Am I guaranteed a pass if I'm on the waitlist?

No. Signing up for the waitlist grabs a spot in line but does not guarantee that passes will be available at a later date.

Can I request more than one type of pass?

You can now add multiple items to your waitlist request! Please note: If your waitlist request is approved, ALL items on the request will be purchased. Please only request what you intend to purchase if your request is offered.

Is there a layaway plan for waitlist passes?

Currently, layaway is not available for waitlist passes. You will be charged full price at time of purchase.

Once I'm on the waitlist, when will waitlist passes be offered?

Waitlist passes will be released as they become available. You will receive the waitlist offer when your turn comes up, should they become available. You are not guaranteed an offer to purchase passes.

Will waitlist passes be mailed to me or will they be placed at will call?

What is the "Sell Tickets, Get Festival Cash" program?

"Sell Tickets, Get Festival Cash" is a referral-based program, in partnership with FEVO, that lets existing ticketbuyers earn $25 of Festival Cash for every ticket sold, regardless of ticket type. After opting into the program on FEVO, users will receive a unique link to share with anyone. Any time someone purchases with your link, you will receive $25 per ticket sold loaded onto your festival wristband. Festival cash earned will only be available for the 2026 festival and limited to purchases onsite during the festival weekend. You must claim the Festival Cash by inputting your Festival wristband ID and then the $25 Festival Cash will be loaded to your Festival wristband.

How do I sign up?

To take part in the "Sell Tickets, Get Festival Cash" referral-based program, you must be an existing Front Gate Tickets Louder Than Life 2026 ticket buyer. You will sign up here and must provide your Front Gate Tickets Order Number to sign up. Follow the instructions after submitting your information to start earning your festival cash.

How can I earn Festival Cash?

Every time someone purchases a ticket using your referral link, you’ll earn $25 of Festival Cash loaded on to your festival wristband that you designate at the end of the referral sales period. The more tickets you sell, the more you will earn.

What kind of rewards can I earn?

The only reward available for this program is Festival Cash, $25 for every ticket sold loaded onto your festival wristband for use only during the 2026 festival weekend.

How do I track my sales and Festival Cash?

After signing up, you will have access to MyFEVO, your personalized dashboard where you can track the number of tickets sold via your personalized link, view your current Festival Cash earned, access additional customer service and track your progress.

Can I share my link on any platform?

Yes! You can share your referral link on social media, through email, text, or even in person. The more creative you are, the more likely you are to earn Festival Cash!

Do I need to be an influencer or have a large following to succeed?

Not at all! While having a large audience can certainly help, anyone can succeed by sharing their link with friends, family, and their personal networks. Being passionate about the festival and sharing it with enthusiasm will go a long way.

Is there a deadline to sign up for the "Sell Tickets, Get Festival Cash" program?

Yes! The deadline to sell tickets and earn Festival Cash is 7/31/26. The deadline to claim your Festival Cash by entering your wristband ID is 7/10/26. Keep these dates in mind to ensure you don't miss out!

Do I have to attend the festival to participate?

While it’s not required to attend the festival to participate in the program, it is the only way to utilize your Festival Cash earned.

Can I participate in the program after I’ve already purchased my ticket?

Yes, as long as the program is still open and you have purchased a valid ticket for Louder Than Life 2026 from Front Gate Tickets, you'll use your Front Gate Tickets order number to join.

What happens if someone cancels their ticket after I refer them?

If a referred customer cancels their ticket or is refunded, the associated sale will be voided, and you will not earn Festival Cash for that sale.

How do I contact customer support if I have issues with my account?

You can contact FEVO's support team by emailing ordersupport@fevo.com or through the contact form on your MyFEVO Dashboard. Their team will be happy to assist you with any questions or issues.

Are there any fees to participate in the "Sell Tickets, Get Festival Cash" program?

No, there are no fees to join the program. All you need to do is purchase your ticket, and you can start sharing your link and earn Festival Cash.

I got an error message when trying to sign up. Why?

You may have received an error because only one email can be used to sign up. Please ensure you’re using a unique email that hasn’t been registered before.

When will I receive my Festival Cash?

Festival Cash will be added to your festival wristband and only available to use for purchases of food, beverage or merchandise at the 2026 festival. You must claim the Festival Cash by inputting your Festival wristband ID and then the $25 Festival Cash will be loaded to your Festival wristband.

How do I load my Festival Cash to my wristband?

Once you receive your wristband in the mail, you will be required to enter your unique Wristband ID on the MyFevo Dashboard. The unique Wristband ID is an alphanumeric code found on the inside of your wristband. Enter it exactly how it is displayed on the wristband.

Is there a limit to how many tickets I can sell?

No, there’s no limit! The more tickets you sell, the more Festival Cash you can earn!

I need a replacement wristband (ie. my wristband is too tight), can my Festival Cash balance transfer to my new wristband?

No, treat your wristband like a gift card. If you lose it, you can replace your wristband to attend the festival, but we will be unable to transfer your Festival Cash to the replacement wristband.

What happens if I don't use all the funds on my wristband? Is the Festival Cash transferrable?

No, the Festival Cash earned is only available to use on-site at the 2026 festival weekend for food, beverage, or festival merchandise and is non-transferable.

I have multiple Front Gate Tickets orders, which one do I use upon registration?

You can use any Louder Than Life Front Gate Tickets Order Number to sign up. More importantly, when you receive your festival wristband, be sure to enter the Wristband ID for the wristband you intend to wear/use during the festival weekend.

I've signed up with my Front Gate Tickets order number. Why am I getting an error message?

Please reach out to ordersupport@fevo.com customer support with your information and they will be glad to look into your specific instance. You may have received an error because only one email can be used to sign up. Please ensure you’re using a unique email that hasn’t been registered before. Reminder, Festival Cash is non-transferable between wristbands.

How do I confirm the Festival Cash balance on my wristband throughout the weekend?

Your earned Festival Cash balance will be updated and can be viewed throughout the referral-based program. Unfortunately, you will not be able to track your remaining credit balance on the dashboard after you've purchased items at the festival.

Can I add additional funds to my wristband?

In addition to selling tickets to earn Festival Cash, you may use the festival's Cashless Registration to add a preferred credit card to your wristband to make purchases at the festival.

Can I use my own Referral to buy tickets?

Yes, however, you won’t earn Fest Cash when you use your own referral link.

I'm at the festival and have issues with the Festival Cash loaded to my wristband. It's not working... where do I go?

Please visit an Info Booth or Guest Services onsite at the festival for any troubleshootng questions pertaining to wristband payments.
