Use **your own exports** (screenshots / short clips / stills) from these as **Video Ingredients** or **start/end frames** in [Google Flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow). Do not upload full copyrighted recaps as-is; extract 1–3 sec moments or still frames you have rights to reference.

## What to export before opening Flow

1. **Wide crowd** — sea of people, late afternoon, Highland grounds feel  
2. **Phone in hand** — POV walking, shaky, festival wristband visible  
3. **Stage lights** — orange/purple haze, silhouette crowd (no artist logos if avoidable)  
4. **Map moment** — official amenity map on phone screen (use `public/maps/ltl-2026-official-amenity-map.png` from this repo)  
5. **ltl26.com UI** — screenshot of `/map` and `/overlay` from production or localhost  

Save to: `G:\ltl-2026\assets\flow-ingredients\` (create folder locally; not committed if large).

## Flow project settings (defaults)

- **Model:** Veo 3 (with audio if available)  
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 primary (TikTok/Reels/Stories), also render 16:9 for YouTube  
- **Duration:** 8s per clip (Flow default); stitch in CapCut / DaVinci  
- **Mode:** Video Ingredients (up to 3 refs) or Video Frames (start/end)
